## Overview
This project aims to construct model portfolios, from a basket of ETFs, for Japanese investors. The ETFs considered include a diverse range of asset classes, such as fixed income, equities, real estate, and commodities. 
Analysis and allocation of portfolio weights were conducted in R using the packages (`fPortfolio`, `PortfolioAnalytics`).


## Suggested ETFs
The following ETFs were considered for the portfolio:

- **BIV**: US Intermediate Bond
- **VCSH**: US Investment Grade Bond
- **BNDX**: Global Bond ETF
- **VNQ**: US REIT
- **JP 1343**: Japanese REIT
- **JP 1555**: Australian REIT
- **SPDR**: US Stock
- **VT**: Global Stock
- **HVST AU**: Equity Dividend ETF AU
